About Me

I am an independent game developer from Colombia. I have always been passionate about creating stories with a strong emotional impact—stories that challenge morality and ethics while exploring complex human experiences and difficult realities.

Under the name SilverSky Studio, I work to transform these stories into video games that encourage players to reflect and connect with the characters. My goal is to create games that leave a lasting impression and inspire both emotion and reflection.

In Development

Why would you be afraid of your own brother?

Project Status

  • Genre: Psychological Horror / Adventure
  • Platform: PC (Itch.io)
  • Status: Active Development
  • Target Demo: 2027

Synopsis

After learning that her brother miraculously survived a suicide attempt, Mehri is forced to cross the city to visit him and stay by his side in the aftermath of the tragedy. Help her make it there and back safely.

⚠️ Content Warning: This game explores heavy narrative themes, including psychological trauma, suicide, and the lasting impacts of sexual abuse inspired by real-life accounts. Player discretion is advised.
